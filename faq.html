Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before booking

1. What areas do you serve?

We proudly operate within a 30-mile radius of Merrifield, VA—covering Northern Virginia (Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Chantilly, Leesburg, Gainesville, Haymarket), Washington, DC, and select Maryland locations (Bethesda, Chevy Chase, Potomac, Rockville, Gaithersburg, Silver Spring, Takoma Park). If your ZIP isn’t listed, reach out—we’re always expanding!

2. How can I verify if you're available in my ZIP code?

Check the Service Area page for ZIP codes. If yours is missing, send us a quick message or use the contact form—we frequently cover adjacent areas and can make exceptions for nearby customers.

3. What exactly is included in a full interior & exterior detail?

Exterior: Hand wash, clay-bar decontamination, multi-stage polishing, wax or ceramic coating, tire shine, and trim rejuvenation.

Interior: Comprehensive vacuuming, shampoo & upholstery cleaning, leather/vinyl conditioning, dashboard & console detailing, odor neutralizing.

Extras (on request): Headlight restoration, engine bay degreasing, pet-hair removal. Every detail is tailored to your vehicle’s type and condition.

4. What if I only want one service, like ceramic coating or paint correction?

No problem! Stand-alone services include:

  • Paint Correction – single or multi-stage scratch/swirl removal
  • Ceramic Coating – durable 3-5 year nano-coating
  • Headlight Restoration, Engine Cleaning, Odor Treatment
Let us know what you need during booking.

5. How long does each service take?

Full interior + exterior detail: 3–4 hours.
Paint correction + ceramic coating: 4–7 hours (luxury/SUVs may take most of the day).
We’ll always provide an estimated window when booking.

6. Do you bring your own water and electricity?

Yes. We carry a fresh-water tank and a quiet generator. We’re fully self-sufficient, but if an outdoor outlet is available we’ll plug in for even quieter operation—perfect for residential neighborhoods or early-morning jobs.

7. Can you remove pet hair, sand, or strong odors from the interior?

Absolutely. We offer specialized pet-hair removal, deep carpet extraction, and ozone-based odor elimination. For severe cases we recommend an interior reset detail using steam and enzyme cleaners.

8. What is ceramic coating and how long does it last?

Ceramic coating is a nano-ceramic layer that bonds with your paint, adding gloss, UV protection, and chemical resistance. With proper maintenance it lasts 3–5 years.

9. How should I maintain the coating at home?

We’ll provide a starter kit and show you how to:
• Wash with a pH-neutral, sulfate-free shampoo.
• Rinse thoroughly and dry with microfiber towels.
• Apply a ceramic-spray booster every 4-6 weeks.
Regular care preserves durability and gloss.

10. What's paint correction and do I need it?

Paint correction removes defects like swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. If your paint looks dull or shows fine scratches, correction restores mirror-like depth—ideal before ceramic coating.

11. Do you offer protective or premium packages?

Yes. Popular upgrades include:
• Two-step paint correction
• 3–5 year ceramic coating
• Monthly/quarterly maintenance plans
• Multi-vehicle discounts for fleets or enthusiast collections
We can tailor any package—just ask!

12. Do you offer mobile detailing during the winter months?

Yes—year-round. In colder weather we focus on interiors, paint protection, and cold-cure ceramic coatings. Heated-water systems let us detail in garages, driveways, or enclosed areas. If extreme cold or snow makes work unsafe, we’ll reschedule at no charge.

13. What if it rains or it's snowy?

Light rain is fine. For heavy rain, storms, or unsafe conditions we’ll reschedule free of charge. Ceramic coatings require dry conditions—if weather starts mid-service we’ll pause or move under cover.
